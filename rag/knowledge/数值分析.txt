主题：迭代法、插值、数值积分与误差。

二分法：若 f(a)f(b)<0，中点 c=(a+b)/2；保留函数值异号的子区间。牛顿法：x_{n+1}=x_n-f(x_n)/f'(x_n)。割线法：x_{n+1}=x_n-f(x_n)(x_n-x_{n-1})/[f(x_n)-f(x_{n-1})]。
不动点迭代 x_{n+1}=g(x_n) 在区间 I 上若 g(I) subset I 且 max_I |g'(x)|<1，则存在唯一不动点并收敛。

拉格朗日插值：p(x)=sum y_i L_i(x)，其中 L_i(x)=product_{j!=i}(x-x_j)/(x_i-x_j)。等距节点时可用差商或待定系数。插值多项式必须逐点核验给定数据。
复化梯形公式：h[(f(a)+f(b))/2+sum_{i=1}^{n-1}f(a+ih)]。前向欧拉法：y_{n+1}=y_n+h f(x_n,y_n)。

Jacobi 与 Gauss-Seidel：若矩阵严格对角占优，则迭代收敛，这是充分条件。若迭代矩阵范数小于 1，则迭代收敛。
泰勒展开应写清展开点与截断阶。Chebyshev 节点为 cos((2k-1)pi/(2n))，有助于减小插值振荡。
条件数大表示输入相对误差可能被放大为较大的解相对误差。

Newton's method uses x_{k+1}=x_k-f(x_k)/f'(x_k). Preserve exact fractions through every requested iterate and substitute the final iterate back into the recurrence as a check.
For an even number n of equal subintervals, composite Simpson quadrature is h/3 times [f(x_0)+f(x_n)+4 sum over odd interior indices+2 sum over even interior indices]. Do not confuse n subintervals with n nodes.
Two-point Gauss-Legendre quadrature on [a,b] uses the two nodes (a+b)/2 plus or minus (b-a)/(2 sqrt(3)), each with weight (b-a)/2. It is exact for every polynomial of degree at most three.
For polynomial interpolation, solve for the unique polynomial of the stated maximum degree or use Lagrange basis polynomials. Verify all supplied data values before differentiating or evaluating the interpolant.
