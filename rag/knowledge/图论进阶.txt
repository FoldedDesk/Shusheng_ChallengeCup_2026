主题：图论与网络的进阶方法。

简单图的度数和为两倍边数。若最小度数 delta(G)>=k，可从任一顶点沿未重复顶点扩展路径；极大简单路径的端点邻居受路径约束，常用于证明长路径存在。
连通图有生成树；树中任意两点间路径唯一。删除树的一条边会使图不连通，添加一条非树边产生唯一圈。

欧拉路要求非零度顶点在同一连通分支，奇度顶点为 0 或 2 个；哈密顿问题不能仅用度数和，应根据题目使用 Dirac、Ore 或归纳插入法。
二部图可用 BFS 染色判定，不含奇圈等价于二部。K_{m,n} 的边数为 mn，左右部交替的长度 r 路径需按位置所在部分类计数。

邻接矩阵的 A^r 给出长度 r 游走数；简单路径还需排除重复顶点。最短路算法中，非负权使用 Dijkstra，允许负边但无负圈可用 Bellman-Ford。
平面图应先确认连通分支数。连通平面简单图面边界长度至少三时 3F<=2E；与 Euler 公式联立可得边数上界。

The Matrix-Tree theorem says that a connected graph's spanning-tree count is any cofactor of its Laplacian. Equivalently, it is the product of all nonzero Laplacian eigenvalues divided by the number of vertices; multiplicities must be included.
For a complete bipartite, complete tripartite, or complete multipartite graph K_{n_1,...,n_k} with N=sum n_i, the spanning-tree count is N^(k-2) product_i (N-n_i)^(n_i-1). In particular tau(K_{m,n})=m^(n-1)n^(m-1). Deleting one edge from K_{m,n} leaves m^(n-2)n^(m-2)(m-1)(n-1); this one-edge formula must not be reused when several edges or a cycle are deleted.
The number of spanning trees of the d-dimensional hypercube Q_d follows from Laplacian eigenvalue 2j with multiplicity C(d,j): tau(Q_d)=[product_{j=1}^d (2j)^{C(d,j)}]/2^d. Check d=1 and d=2 before evaluating a larger case.
For labeled trees with prescribed degrees d_i, Prufer codes give (n-2)!/product_i(d_i-1)! when every degree is fixed. With only some degrees fixed, count Prufer words with the corresponding symbol multiplicities and sum over the unrestricted symbols.
