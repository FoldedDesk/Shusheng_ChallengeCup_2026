主题：整除、同余、有限域与伽罗瓦基础。

Euclid 算法计算 gcd(a,b)，并可写出 Bezout 恒等式 ax+by=gcd(a,b)。若 gcd(a,m)=1，则 a 在模 m 下可逆。
中国剩余定理：模数两两互素时，同余方程组有唯一模乘积的解。Euler 定理 a^phi(n)=1 需要 gcd(a,n)=1；Fermat 小定理是素数模数特例。

多项式除法与 gcd 在域上成立。不可约多项式生成的商环是域；有限域 F_{p^n} 的非零元构成循环乘法群。
群作用的轨道-稳定子定理：|Gx|=|G|/|G_x|。类方程可用于 p 群中心非平凡等结论。

环同态的核是理想，第一同构定理 R/ker phi 同构于 im phi。证明同构需验证良定义、同态性、单射和满射。

For x^2 congruent to 1 modulo an odd prime power p^a there are exactly two roots. Modulo 2 there is one root, modulo 4 two roots, and modulo 2^a for a>=3 four roots. Combine prime-power roots by CRT, then count complete residue periods plus the bounded remainder when a range is imposed.
For a factorial quotient or multinomial, compute every prime valuation with Legendre's formula v_p(n!)=sum_{j>=1} floor(n/p^j). A power of a composite base divides it only up to the minimum valuation divided by the corresponding base exponent.
The equation 1/x+1/y=1/n is equivalent to (x-n)(y-n)=n^2. Positive ordered or unordered solutions therefore reduce to divisors of n^2, with the symmetry convention checked explicitly.
To count integers with a prescribed divisor number, write n=product p_i^{a_i}, enumerate multiplicative partitions product(a_i+1)=d, and assign larger exponents to smaller primes when minimizing or bounding n.
For Pell-type equations, derive solutions from powers of the fundamental unit and prove monotone growth before truncating at a stated bound. A numerical search without an exhaustiveness argument is not a certificate.
